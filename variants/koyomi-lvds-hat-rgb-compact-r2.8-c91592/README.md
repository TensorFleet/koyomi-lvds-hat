# Koyomi RGB/DPI LVDS — compact R2.8 C91592 production candidate

R2.8 is the sourcing-ready successor to immutable R2.7. Its outline, routing,
connector identities, pin maps, and top-only fitted set are unchanged. The
only electrical BOM change is FL1/FL2 from Murata `DLP2ADN121HL4L` / JLCPCB
`C710576` to the same-series `DLP2ADN900HL4L` / `C91592` substitution used on
the previous five-board JLCPCB build on 2026-07-30.

## Mechanical target

- R2.6 outline: **65.0 × 28.25 mm = 1836.25 mm²**.
- R2.8 base rectangle: **58.0 × 22.2 mm = 1287.6 mm²**.
- `JFFC1` support tab: **20.0 × 0.5 mm = 10.0 mm²**.
- Actual board area: **1297.6 mm²**, a **29.33% reduction**.
- Copper: four layers (`F.Cu`, `In1.Cu`, `In2.Cu`, `B.Cu`).
- Assembly: **23 fitted parts, all on the top side**.
- Four mounting holes retained.

The three production cable connectors remain at board edges: `JFFC1` for the
system harness, `J2` for the panel, and `JBL1` for backlight/sideband support.
All three cable openings face their assigned board edge. `JFFC1` is rotated
180° from the first floorplan and sits behind a shallow local support tab so
its shield pads retain edge clearance. Their authoritative
edge and rotation record is `edge-connector-orientations.json`; a connector
opening toward the board interior blocks routing and fabrication.

## Status

This revision is fully routed and approved as a **fabrication-ready
prototype**. Native KiCad reports zero electrical/clearance errors and zero
unconnected pads; errors-only ERC is also zero. The routed interface,
straight-through system interconnect, connector placement, and top-only
assembly gates pass. The reproducible JLCPCB package is generated by
`../../scripts/build_r2_8_jlc_candidate.py` into
`../../fab/r2.8-compact-rgb-production-jlc-c91592/`.

As checked on 2026-08-20, `J2` (`C5311655`) has zero warehouse stock and a
442-piece pre-order minimum. `FL1`/`FL2` now use in-stock `C91592` (3874 pieces
at the check). `J2` remains the exact panel-mating connector and must not be
silently substituted. See `FABRICATION_STATUS.md` for release evidence and the
remaining procurement boundary.

The inherited `scripts/make_r2_7_floorplan.py` and `scripts/finish_r2_7.py`
record the unchanged R2.7 geometry and routing ancestry. Fresh R2.8 KiCad
renders and reports live in `renders/` and `reports/`.
