# Koyomi RGB/DPI LVDS — compact R2.9 silkscreen-clearance production candidate

R2.9 is the manufacturing-cleanup successor to immutable R2.8. Its outline,
routing, connector identities, pin maps, electrical BOM, and top-only fitted
set are unchanged. The only layout change is silkscreen cleanup around the
fine-pitch panel connector `J2`: overlapping `C2`, `C3`, `C4`, `C5`, and
`FL2` references are hidden, and silkscreen-over-copper plus
silkscreen-to-edge checks are promoted to release-blocking errors.

## Mechanical target

- R2.6 outline: **65.0 × 28.25 mm = 1836.25 mm²**.
- R2.9 base rectangle: **58.0 × 22.2 mm = 1287.6 mm²**.
- `JFFC1` support tab: **20.0 × 0.5 mm = 10.0 mm²**.
- Actual board area: **1297.6 mm²**, a **29.33% reduction**.
- Copper: four layers (`F.Cu`, `In1.Cu`, `In2.Cu`, `B.Cu`).
- Assembly: **23 fitted parts, all on the top side**.
- Four mounting holes retained.

The three production cable connectors remain at board edges: `JFFC1` for the
system harness, `J2` for the panel, and `JBL1` for backlight/sideband support.
All three cable openings face their assigned board edge. `JFFC1` is rotated
180° from the first floorplan and sits behind a shallow local support tab so
its shield pads retain edge clearance. Their authoritative
edge and rotation record is `edge-connector-orientations.json`; a connector
opening toward the board interior blocks routing and fabrication.

## Status

This revision is fully routed and **board-source ready**, but its fabrication
package is currently blocked by the repository-wide LCD interconnect gate.
Native KiCad reports zero electrical/clearance errors and zero
unconnected pads; errors-only ERC is also zero. The routed interface,
straight-through system interconnect, connector placement, and top-only
assembly board-local gates pass. Once the system gate is cleared, the
reproducible JLCPCB package is generated by
`../../scripts/build_r2_9_jlc_candidate.py` into
`../../fab/r2.9-compact-rgb-production-jlc-c91592/`.

The blocking system contract is `gpio-breakout-lcd`: its proposed ES&S
FH41-compatible cable still lacks public confirmation of the shield/ground
plate construction required by Hirose FH41. Do not generate or upload an R2.9
fabrication package until `check_interconnect_release.py --board lcd` passes.

As checked on 2026-08-20, `J2` (`C5311655`) has zero warehouse stock and a
442-piece pre-order minimum. `FL1`/`FL2` now use in-stock `C91592` (3874 pieces
at the check). `J2` remains the exact panel-mating connector and must not be
silently substituted. See `FABRICATION_STATUS.md` for release evidence and the
remaining procurement boundary.

The inherited `scripts/make_r2_7_floorplan.py` and `scripts/finish_r2_7.py`
record the unchanged R2.7 geometry and routing ancestry. Fresh R2.9 KiCad
renders and reports live in `renders/` and `reports/`.
